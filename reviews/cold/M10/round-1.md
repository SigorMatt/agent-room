## Scope

These go beyond the spec, and each is harmless or helpful. `test_gate.py` can now be imported, and its `verdict()` helper checks and then strips the FAIL head. room.py's `startswith` check is stricter than the spec's `rev-parse --verify`. ARCHITECT.md adds a `git fetch origin pull/<PR>/head` step and a pointer to the 5.3 clone. `docs/design/m3-reviews-gate.md` still shows the old form `MERGE-HOLD-<n>: PR #<PR>, reason 5 (sensitive paths): <path>, …` and the old `gate: FAIL hold reasons <list>`. It is a historical spec, and the M6f spec didn't ask for it to change. The spec's own limits leave some holds unbound to a head. A lead-judged reason-5 hold for weakened tests at paths the gate doesn't match is one. A reason-1 owner decision answered through a `MERGE-HOLD` (`RoomLog.answers`) is another. The spec doesn't say what a hold that names several heads means, and LEAD.md doesn't forbid one (R1-1). I found no way to merge sensitive code that no hold showed.
