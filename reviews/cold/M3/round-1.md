## Scope

Outside the spec, all small: the exempt set accepts `round-<r>` for any `r` where the spec says 1 or 2 (the code comment justifies it by an extra round a DECISION may grant); `--allow-hold` also accepts an untagged trusted comment from Matthew, not only an architect comment; the WAIT line is marked `…` instead of `✓`/`✗`; `merge` also takes `--mission`; the PR check also rejects a head in a fork; cold reports are validated beyond the spec (ID prefix matches the file, severity is high, medium or low, no duplicate IDs); and `merge` clears the intent on 409 and 405, where the spec says nothing. New `templates/reviews/` files hold the four record formats, and the doc points to them. Missing from the diff: v1's "every finding gets a disposition" is not enforced for Qodo findings absent from the latest fetch (R1-5); the playbook templates were not aligned where their globs fall short of what they intend (R1-1); and the LEDGER template header still says `Source review` where the spec has `source`, which is cosmetic because the gate reads only the ID column. Nothing in the diff touches M2 behaviour except the extended fake `gh`. `is_ancestor` is redefined at room.py:1498 with the same semantics as M2's at room.py:704, which is harmless.
