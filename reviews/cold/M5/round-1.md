## Scope

Outside the spec:
- room.py gains a preflight `✗` when "Allow merge commits" is off, with a test and a fake-gh default.
- `wait`'s default timeout drops from 1740 to 540 seconds, and the `--from`/`--cursor` error text changes.
- `templates/playbook/PLAYBOOK.md` gets an environment-needs comment.
- `BUILD-STATE.md` gets finding J and marks M4 done.
- `v1.md` itself is edited: the ACK window goes from two minutes to 30. The spec names `v1.md` as the authority and asks for disagreements to be raised in the PR, not edited away.

Deviations from the spec:
- LEAD.md takes the lease before acting on reconcile's hints, which is needed because the hints write state.
- LEAD.md deletes the watchdog before `DONE`, against the spec's order (R1-4).
- The phase-end and drill rules miss v1's drill outcomes (R1-2).

Missing from the spec's coverage:
- How a resumed lead treats an already-answered `PAUSE`/`STOP` (R1-1), and the cross-phase cursor (R1-3).
- Budget extension after a decision (R1-7).
- How the review-record templates reach the lead at run time (R1-11).
