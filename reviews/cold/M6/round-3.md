## Scope

Outside the spec but in keeping with it: `join-line` exits 6 and preflight fails on a pin the prompt rejects; `handoff` prints the `start-lead` hint; `start_refusal` ignores a halted status recorded for another room; LEAD.md reads the room on every subagent wake (the spec lists only step boundaries and foreground waits) and greps cold reports for tokens; every brief carries a secrets rule; ARCHITECT.md adds `LEAD-STARTED: by hand`, the unconfirmed fire-action variant, the 30-minute rule for its own starts, and the deleted-lead-task branch. Nothing the spec asks for is missing from the code, the protocols or the manuals. Stale or contradictory text the spec's final form didn't clean up: the spec's own `start-lead` section still lists `watchdog` as a `ROLE`, against its message kinds (R3-5); the v1.md note and BUILD-STATE's M6b row still describe watchdog takeovers (R3-6); and A-10's M6c plan depends on them (R3-2). The spec's design leaves three gaps that the code follows faithfully. A decision counts as answered by any lead message after it, even when no lead applied it (R3-1). A parked room is quiet even when its escalation's buzz failed (R3-8). The `state unreadable` alarm is listed under stalls but gets none of the stalls' reminders (R3-4). The B-5 removal also took S2-5's `start-lead` test with it (R3-3). All seven suites pass.
