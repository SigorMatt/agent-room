## Scope

The focus diff stays inside the spec's revised "When a start is due". It adds episodes (from the later of the progress time and the newest architect `LEAD-STARTED`; the whole room for pre-start), the silent-after hold-off from the last sign of a lead, at most two takeover starts, then one final alarm, decision starts that don't count, and the three-alarm cap per episode. It also adds the 30-minute window, the own-lease refusal text, the decision-start suffix, the secrets rule in every brief with the grep before committing a cold report, and the R2-6 docs. Nothing in it goes beyond the spec. One gap against the spec: the code posts an episode's first alarm before its first start is due, while the spec says the two come together (S1-3). The docs overstate the episode rule in two places. They promise that any start by the architect reopens watching, which is false before any lead has joined (S1-1). They also assume every by-hand start leaves a `LEAD-STARTED`, which no step makes happen for **Run now** or the Code tab (S1-2, S1-4). The texts quoted in LEAD.md, ARCHITECT.md and the manuals match room.py's exact output. The watchdog has no loop: each episode allows at most two starts, and only progress or the architect opens a new one. No new path puts a token in output, room text or a committed file. All seven suites pass, and mutating each focus guard (M1 to M7) makes test_relay.py fail.
