## Scope

The focus diff stays within the six Qodo findings and S1-1 to S1-5, and each spec change is marked `(qodo PR #11)` or `(cold S1-n)`. It adds: an unconfirmed start outcome (-1 from `fire()`, posted as `LEAD-STARTED: unconfirmed …`, exit 0); the token through curl's stdin; retries of the `LEAD-STARTED` post; up to two decision starts, then one alarm; the takeover hold-off on any lead message; the pre-start architect opener; the `LEAD ONLINE` opener after a final alarm; the held-off note; and the matching docs. Nothing in it goes beyond the spec. The quoted texts in LEAD.md, ARCHITECT.md and the manuals match the code's exact strings (the unconfirmed `LEAD-STARTED`, `no lead joined after DECISION-<n> (step …)`, `start held off: a lead showed signs at <time>`), apart from the held-off note's "ends" and its "started or joined" (S1-3 row). The watchdog has no start loop. Decision starts stop at two per decision, and takeovers at two per episode. A new episode opens only on an architect `LEAD-STARTED` or on a lead's `LEAD ONLINE` after a final alarm, both of which need a real start (expD.py: 4 starts over 61h, then quiet). No new path puts the token in argv, output, room text or a committed file. The main problem is S2-1. The hold-off that protects a working lead also blocks the second-start alarm, so a takeover lead that loops with `STATUS` is never alarmed, contradicting the spec's "alarms are unchanged". The decision-retry fix still leaves two silent paths: starts that fail (S2-2), and a start by hand after its final alarm (S2-3). All seven suites pass, and each of ten mutations of the focus guards (M1 to M10) makes test_relay.py fail.
