## Scope

The focus diff stays within S2-1 to S2-6, and each change is marked `(cold S2-n)` in the code, the spec and the docs. The core changes: alarms no longer depend on starts (first alarm, second-start alarm, final alarm, a reminder every 4 × silent-after, a cap of three per episode of every kind); the decision path becomes an episode with the same rules; an alarm due in the same run as a start now goes out through an outcome callback, so it can name a failed start; `post_quietly` checks for a landed copy before retrying; and gateway errors become unconfirmed starts. Where the spec, ARCHITECT.md and the manuals quote a text, it matches the code's string: the stall path's first, second-start, final and reminder alarms, the decision path's first and final alarms, `(last start failed: …)` and `(start held off: …)`. The exceptions are the decision path's second-start alarm and the undocumented pre-start reminder (S3-5). No new path puts the token in argv, output or room text: the new `(last start failed: …)` note posts reasons that `start_lead` already scrubs (exp6). Recorded starts stay bounded at two per episode (an unrecorded one is S3-1), and new episodes open only on an architect `LEAD-STARTED` or on a lead `LEAD ONLINE` after a final alarm. A healthy parked room, with or without an answered decision, stayed quiet for 43h (exp5). The weak points come with the new structure. The outcome callback posts the alarm between the fire and the `LEAD-STARTED`, so a GitHub error there loses the start record (S3-1). The second-start branch shadows the reminder and the decision path's first alarm (S3-2). And the cap of three, which now counts reminders and, for an R1-1 stall, the decision path's alarms, can leave a room with no lead, no starts left and no final alarm (S3-3, S3-4). All seven suites pass. Each of six mutations of the focus guards (M1 to M6, /tmp/cold-m6b-s3/mut/) makes test_relay.py fail.
