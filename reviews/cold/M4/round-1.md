## Scope

Outside the spec: `state set` validates `status` and `mission_started`; `reconcile` adds a `status` line with a resume hint and invents its own intent grammar, of which only `merge PR #<n> at <sha>` is written by room.py (by M3's `merge`), while `open PR …`, `post <KIND>` and `close room` appear nowhere else yet, so LEAD.md (M5) has to use them word for word; `HANDOFF` gains a second paragraph ("being scheduled now") because it is posted before scheduling and can't say whether a successor was scheduled, as v1 asks; `doc_url` takes a version, `lease_holder` is refactored into `pushed_state`, and `fake_gh_state.py` gains the PR list and commit endpoints. Missing from the diff, or thin: nothing returns `status` to `running`, and nothing records `mission_started` per mission, both being left to the lead (R1-2); v1's "record before acting" for scheduling a successor isn't covered, since `handoff` clears `intent` before the agent schedules and reconcile can't judge such an intent; there are no tests for `handoff` without a checkout (it works: E10), the watchdog's error exit 1, the 404/422 race on a deleted branch, or `drill: no-handoff`; and the base predates later M2 fixes that M4 relies on (d938e1a, R1-7) and the finding G and H notes, so M4 needs a rebase before merge.
