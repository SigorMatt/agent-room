## Scope

Outside the spec but in keeping with it:
- `start_refusal` skips the `done`/`paused`/`stopped` guard when the machine block's `room` names another room (M4's rule for statuses).
- Preflight adds the missing parts in parentheses.
- The README role table, glossary, files-and-owners, troubleshooting and move/upgrade pages are updated.
- fake_curl.py is extended for the fire endpoint.

The diff also removes two pre-M6b safeguards the spec didn't ask to remove: ARCHITECT.md 9.5's "a lead that waits on Matthew and doesn't answer" bullet, and the old 9.6 step 4 `waiting for owner` check, with its troubleshooting row. That removal is behind R1-1.

Everything in the spec's room.py, LEAD.md, ARCHITECT.md, Manuals and Also sections is present. From its Tests list, only `--no-start` on the decision path is untested, and "never after a state unreadable alarm" (covered by the spec's "all covered by tests") has no test.

The spec itself leaves gaps that the code follows faithfully:
- no start or alarm while the lease is held at `waiting for owner` (R1-1);
- once per stall, judged only by watchdog `LEAD-STARTED` (R1-2);
- room reads only at step boundaries (R1-3);
- `done` refused regardless of the lease (R1-5).
